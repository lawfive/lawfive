# Devices (DEV)

The Garmin watches the Garmin app runs on, and what their buttons and screens mean for its controls. Wear OS and Apple Watch are covered in [wear-os-and-apple-watch.md](wear-os-and-apple-watch.md).

## Watches

- **DEV-1** The Garmin app runs on the Forerunner 965 and 265, the fēnix 7 and 8, the epix (Gen 2) and the Venu series.
- **DEV-2** Every one of them has a touchscreen, but the Venu series has only 2 or 3 buttons. So every control works by touch, and the essential match controls also work with the Venu's buttons (INP-5).

## Status

Everything above is built: 28 Garmin models, every control by touch, and the match's essentials by the Venu's buttons.
