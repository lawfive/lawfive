# Devices (DEV)

The Garmin watches the Garmin app runs on, and what their buttons and screens mean for its controls. Wear OS and Apple Watch are covered in [wear-os-and-apple-watch.md](wear-os-and-apple-watch.md).

## Watches

- **DEV-1** The Garmin app runs on Garmin's watches with at least two buttons and Connect IQ 3.2 or later that give a watch app enough memory: the Forerunner 70, 165, 170, 245 Music, 255, 265, 570, 645 Music, 745, 945, 955, 965 and 970, the fēnix 5 Plus, 6 Pro, 7, 8, 9 and E, the epix (Gen 2), the Enduro 3, the Instinct 3 AMOLED and Instinct Crossover AMOLED, the vívoactive 5 and 6, the Venu series, the MARQ, and their D2, Approach and Descent editions. Watches that give an app 128 KB or less (the plain fēnix 6, the Forerunner 245 and 645, the Instinct 2) can't hold it.
- **DEV-2** Most have a touchscreen. Some don't (the fēnix 5 Plus and 6 Pro, the Forerunner 245 Music, 255, 645 Music, 745 and 945, the first MARQ, the Descent Mk2 and the Instinct AMOLEDs), and the Venu series and the other two-button watches (vívoactive, Approach, D2 Air X10) have only 2 or 3 buttons. So every control works by touch on a touchscreen and by the buttons on a five-button watch, and the essential match controls also work with the two-button watches' buttons (INP-5).

## Status

Everything above is built: 90 Garmin models (137 builds with their regional versions), every control by touch on a touchscreen and by the buttons on a five-button watch, and the match's essentials by the two-button watches' buttons. Only the Venu 2 has run a real match; the rest are built and unit-tested in the simulator (R-6). The Connect IQ 3 watches allow 253 top-level names in an app, and LawFive is about 34 under that.
