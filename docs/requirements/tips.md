# Did you know (TIP)

Tips about what LawFive can already do, so people find it without having to read about it. They show on the website and in the Android and iPhone apps; the watch's are for later.

- **TIP-1** **A tip now and then**, on the Matches page under the week's figures: a card with a small picture, "Did you know", a title and a line, then **Got it** and **Show me**.
  - Show me opens the few steps (a dialog on the website, a bottom sheet on the phones), with Got it there too.
  - Only one shows at a time. None on a list opened from Stats, and on the phones none while searching.
  - The tips: mapping the pitch from the watch (TRK-13), the coin toss (PRE-9), kickoff direction (PRE-8; it says it's for watches with a compatible compass), Undo (INP-6), the second yellow (EVT-5, EVT-6), marking a fee paid on the watch (SYNC-11), the match report (DATA-6), the calendar feed (CAL-1), Replay (TRK-10), and keeping messages off the watch during a match (through the watch's own workout settings, as each match is a workout, ACT-1).
  - Undo, the second yellow, the match report and Replay wait until a match has been played.
- **TIP-2** **Rarely, and at random**: an 8% chance each time LawFive is opened (a browser tab's visit; the phone app starting), held until it's next opened so a tip doesn't come and go as pages change. On the website there's none while Get ready shows (WEB-32), nor for the rest of a visit that opened with it.
- **TIP-3** **Got it** retires that tip for good on that browser or phone (kept on the device, not the account; a phone keeps it through signing out). Closing Show me only closes it. Once every tip is retired, none show.
- **TIP-4 (Later)** On the watch: a **Did you know?** row at the end of a downloaded match's screen (UI-19), opening one short tip with Got it. Never during a match.
