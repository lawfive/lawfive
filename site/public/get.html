Get the app

Get LawFive.

On your iPhone, your Android phone and your Garmin watch, with Apple Watch and Wear OS on the way. One free account works on all of them.

Account iPhone Android Garmin

Your account

Free

Make one at app.lawfive.app with your email and a password. It's ready as soon as you make it, and the same account signs in on the website, both phones and every watch. The phone apps need one. A Garmin watch runs matches without one, and only needs it to sync.

iPhone

Early testing

And Apple Watch, through Apple's TestFlight.

You need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later
  • For the watch app, an Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later, paired with it
  • A LawFive account
  1. Open the invitation on your iPhoneOpen LawFive's TestFlight invitation on the iPhone itself.
  2. Get TestFlightIf the page asks, install Apple's free TestFlight app from the App Store, then open the invitation again.
  3. Accept and installIn TestFlight, tap Accept, then Install. LawFive appears on your Home Screen.
  4. Sign inOpen LawFive and sign in with your account's email and password.

On your Apple Watch. The watch app comes with it. If it isn't on the watch, open the Watch app on your iPhone and tap Install beside LawFive. Then, in LawFive on the iPhone, open Link a watch and choose Link your Apple Watch.

Updates. TestFlight brings each new version. Tell us what works and what doesn't from TestFlight, with a screenshot, or at support@lawfive.app.

Android

In testing

And Wear OS, through a test on Google Play.

You need

  • An Android phone with Android 10 or later and Google Play
  • The Google account you use for Google Play on it
  • For the watch app, a watch with Wear OS 3 or later
  • A LawFive account
  1. Join the testers groupOpen the LawFive testers group, signed in with that Google account, and choose Join group.
  2. Become a testerOpen the test's page with the same account and choose Become a tester.
  3. Install from Google PlayFollow the page's link to Google Play and install LawFive. It can take a few minutes after you join to show there.
  4. Sign inOpen LawFive and sign in with your account's email and password.

On your Wear OS watch. Once you're a tester, open Google Play on the watch and install LawFive. Then, on the watch, go to Settings → Account → Link account, and enter the code it shows in Link a watch on your phone.

With a Garmin. The Android app can also carry your Garmin's sync when there's no signal at the field.

Garmin

Connect IQ Store

On 90 Garmin watches, from Garmin's Connect IQ Store.

You need

  • A Garmin watch LawFive runs on: Forerunner, fēnix, epix, Venu, vívoactive and more (the full list)
  • Garmin Connect on your phone, iPhone or Android
  • A LawFive account only to sync: the watch runs matches without one
  1. Open LawFive in the storeOpen LawFive's page in the Connect IQ Store, or search for LawFive in the Connect IQ Store on your phone, signed in with your Garmin account.
  2. Install itChoose Install and pick your watch. It goes to the watch the next time the watch syncs with your phone.
  3. Run a matchLawFive is in the watch's list of activities and apps. Open it and start a match: no account needed.
  4. Link your account, to syncOn the watch, go to Settings → Account → Link account → Get a code. Enter the code at app.lawfive.app/link, or in Link a watch in the LawFive phone app.

Syncing. Once linked, the watch downloads your next matches and sends each one you play, through your phone when it's nearby, even with no signal at the field. How it syncs.

Settings in Garmin Connect. Team names and colors and the match format can also be set from your phone, in Garmin Connect, under LawFive's settings.
