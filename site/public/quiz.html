The Laws quiz

How well do you know the Laws?

Test yourself between matches, at your desk or at the ground, and find out which Laws to read again. Take it here, no account needed.

Pick how hard, and how many

120 questions, a third each easy, medium and hard: the basics of the Laws, the ones for referees with a few seasons in, and the ones that catch people out. Or mix them. Choose 5, 10, 15 or 20, and each go draws new questions in a new order.

Every answer explained

Check your answer and see what was right and why, for the right options and the one you picked, with the Law and section it comes from, linked. Some questions have more than one right answer, and say so.

See where you stand

The end shows your score and how each Law went, weakest first, linking to each, and what you missed with the right answers. Then go again with new questions.

Anywhere

On the website and in the Android and iPhone apps. The phones keep the questions, so it works at a ground with no signal. The questions are written for LawFive from the current Laws of the Game; they aren't official and aren't endorsed by The IFAB.
