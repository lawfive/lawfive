Your season

Everything around the match, in one place.

Your schedule, the grounds, the crew and what you're owed, on the website and your phone, with or without signal.

Matches in, without typing

Add matches by hand or from a template, or import a league's or assignor's schedule from a spreadsheet (CSV or Excel) and import it again when it changes: the same matches update rather than doubling. Apps that send your assignments can add and change matches through LawFive's API, and on Android your phone tells you when they do: a new match, a new kickoff, another venue, or a cancellation.

Never late

  • Reminders on your phone, minutes before kickoff, the day before or the morning of. On Android, the last hours count down to kickoff.
  • Your calendar: a private feed for Apple, Google or Outlook, with the teams, your role, the venue and pitch, the crew and the forecast, starting early enough to get there.
  • Directions to the right pitch, not just the venue, in your maps app.

The weather before you leave

Every kickoff at a venue on the map gets its forecast from about two weeks out, with a warning for a wet field, storms, snow or heat. Turn on notifications and your phone tells you when a new risk appears in the three days before a match. Played matches keep the weather they were played in.

Venues, pitches and the crew

Each ground on a map with its numbered pitches and the parking, and photos. The referees and assistants you work with, how many matches you've done together, and how to reach them, filled in as you add them to matches.

Questions

Fees, reports and your record

  • Fees: what each match pays, what you're owed, and paid or not, marked from the watch too.
  • The match report, ready to print or copy as text, with the goals, cards, injuries and times.
  • A share link for a match, when you want someone to see it.
  • Stats: games by role, competition and venue, distance and speed, across as many seasons as you like.
  • Yours to take: download everything, or your matches as a spreadsheet, at any time.
