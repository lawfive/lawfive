Where you ran

See every match you ran.

Your GPS track and heart rate from each match, worked into the figures referees care about, on the pitch where it happened.

The figures

  • Distance and top speed, for the match and each period.
  • Time in five speed bands, from walking to sprinting, and how many sprints. Set your own sprint speed and the bands follow.
  • Heart rate: average, highest and time in each zone, from the maximum you set.

They're worked out again each time you look, so a better calculation applies to old matches too.

Mark the pitch

Stand on a corner, press Start and hold still for a few seconds while the watch takes its position, then the others. Your run then lands on the real pitch. Without corners, LawFive finds the pitch from how you moved, by your role, and you can adjust it on the map.

On Garmin, Wear OS and Apple Watch

Heatmaps

Where on the pitch your time went, for the match and each period. Across a season, Stats shows a heatmap of every game on one standard pitch, one for games in the middle and one for games on the line, never mixed, and how your time splits across the thirds of the field.

Match replay

Watch your match back. Your run plays out on the pitch with the clock, score, heart rate and speed at every moment, and pauses on each goal and card. Drag to any point, or jump straight to an event. Website, Android and iPhone

From any watch

LawFive's watch apps record their own track, about once a second, linked or not, and sends it with the match. Using another watch or app? Drop its activity file (FIT, GPX or TCX, from Garmin Connect for example) on a played match on the website, and it's converted in your browser and previewed before it's saved.
